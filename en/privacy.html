Thank you for using 72tool.com (the “Site”). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle data.

1. Local processing — files are not uploaded

All tools run locally in your browser. Files you upload or text you enter are processed and computed on your device; they are never uploaded to our servers, and we do not store your file contents.

2. Cookies (loaded only after explicit consent)

We use cookies to remember your preferences (such as language). We load advertising cookies (e.g. Google ads) only after you click “Accept”. Browsing does not imply consent—whether ads are served depends on your explicit choice. You can withdraw consent at any time via the “Reset cookie preferences” button below, or manage/delete cookies in your browser.

3. Ads & third-party services

We display ads through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. Where you have consented, these networks may collect non-personal information (such as device type and approximate region) based on your activity to serve ads, subject to each network’s own privacy policy. We never provide your files or personal identity data to advertisers.

4. GDPR (EU / UK users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, under the GDPR / UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal basis for advertising cookies is your consent (opt-in). To exercise any of these rights or for questions, email easternscrolls@gmail.com and we will respond within the statutory timeframe.

5. CCPA / CPRA (California residents)

For California residents, under the CCPA / CPRA: we do not sell or share your personal information (“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information”). You have the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To exercise these rights, email the address above with “CCPA request” in the subject.

6. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: easternscrolls@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time; the version shown on the Site governs.
