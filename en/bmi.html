FAQ
Q1: What is BMI?
A: Body Mass Index — a simple measure of body fat based on height and weight.
Q2: Is my data uploaded?
A: No, all calculation runs locally in your browser.
Q3: What do the categories mean?
A: Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5–24), Overweight (≥24) under the Chinese standard.
Q4: Is BMI accurate for everyone?
A: It's a general indicator; people with high muscle mass may read higher.
